## Limits and follow-up

No missing, duplicate or non-IG1 safeguard was identified in the 56-item inventory. No catalog IDs, assessment IDs or framework version changed. Existing source URLs use `latest`; they currently identify v8.1 but are not immutable snapshots. This dated ledger and Git history preserve validation provenance, not an archived licensed publication. Source content may change and must be rechecked before a future catalog upgrade.

Cross-framework mappings involving CIS are the three existing PARTIAL relationships to NIST PR.AA-05, PR.DS-11 and PR.AT-01. Both sides were compared against [NIST CSWP 29, Appendix A, printed page 20](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/CSWP/NIST.CSWP.29.pdf) (2024-02-26; accessed 2026-09-22). PARTIAL is justified: granting access does not establish the broader entitlement outcome; automated backups do not establish protection and testing; an awareness program supports but does not itself demonstrate personnel capability. No equivalence or automatic completion is asserted. The full NIST catalog remains Phase 2 work.

QA and phase completion are recorded in the parent report; an item-content conclusion does not by itself complete the Phase 1 QA gate.
