## Cadence matrix

C = management-designed operational cadence; D = Omnisciente Review recommendation; E = event-driven execution. The catalog currently distinguishes these from a universal annual or quarterly mandate. An engagement observation period is neither a control frequency nor a scheduled governance Review. Exact criterion qualifications remain subject to S01 access, so no source-minimum certification is made.

An annual vulnerability-governance Review must not be interpreted as annual scanning or remediation. A quarterly access Review does not replace event-driven provisioning/removal. Backup operations and incident response must follow their actual control design. Preserve these distinctions; management must approve appropriate frequency. No universal sample size, evidence population or 6/12-month audit mandate was added.
