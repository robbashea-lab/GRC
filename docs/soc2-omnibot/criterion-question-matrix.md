## Answer-field and decision contract

Stable catalog IDs identify answers. Missing type means substantive `select`, with Yes / Partially / No / Not sure. Each active select/context needs a response; Not sure is valid. Optional question-detail fields explain actual practice, who/how/coverage/exceptions or alternatives. Missing optional names, document titles, tools, or notes never create deficiencies. The two `text` prompts are optional context, not readiness inputs.

Context uses Relevant / Outside the selected system / Not sure. Relevant activates only the listed children. Outside requires an actual context-detail explanation. Not sure preserves material uncertainty while children are inactive. Retain inactive answers as history but exclude them from current outcome, progress requirements, and practice claims. Skip empty conditional groups. Neither a context answer nor outsourcing creates whole-criterion N/A or readiness by itself. Native N/A remains an explicit manual judgment and rationale under the existing contract.

**R — non-score proposal (all criteria):** active substantive No proposes `needs_attention` (Needs Remediation / Validation), identifying the actual topic and supported remediation, not an unrelated required tool. Partially or material Not sure proposes `in_progress` (Partially Addressed) when meaningful practice is reported, otherwise `not_assessed` (Not Assessed). All active substantive Yes and resolved context may propose `addressed` (Addressed (Readiness)), based only on reported implementation. Missing answers or no meaningful information cannot establish readiness. No automatic `not_applicable` proposal. Attachments, checklist completion, or a count of Yes responses do not establish evidence verification or an auditor conclusion.

**C — conditional rule:** unresolved context or missing outside-context explanation cannot silently produce Addressed. Children affect outcomes only when Relevant. Resolve context and inspect its explanation before relying on reported readiness. Confirmation needs are not automatically confirmed deficiencies. Unresolved alternatives remain confirmation needs, not presumed adequate treatment; particularly for Privacy roles/consent.

The engine owns bounded validation and detail-field naming. Its proposal uses active structured select/context answers, not keyword interpretation of optional prose. Optional detail, names, text prompts, evidence notes, and separate `gaps` / `unknowns` notes are retained as explanatory content; adding or changing them alone does not change proposed status. The required outside-system context explanation is not an optional note and remains part of context validation. Record a substantive deficiency or material uncertainty in the corresponding structured answer. A Yes paired with a note describing an unfulfilled required action is inconsistent and needs operator correction before relying on the proposal; the engine does not resolve that contradiction by analyzing free text. Missing optional metadata is not a deficiency. Do not infer interview answers from legacy text/checklist selections.

## Write-up mapping

One editable Current Implementation uses `OVERVIEW`, `IMPLEMENTATION BREAKDOWN`, and `ITEMS TO ADDRESS` with blank lines and concise bullets. OVERVIEW uses the criterion identity and actual reported context, not assumed client facts. IMPLEMENTATION BREAKDOWN groups active answers and useful details by their topics, separating reported practices, confirmation needs, and confirmed deficiencies; separate free-text gap/unknown notes appear as additional reported context. ITEMS TO ADDRESS uses authored remediation for structured substantive gaps and targeted confirmation for structured unknowns. Optional context enriches prose without becoming a required tool-name assertion or independently generating an outcome. Avoid questionnaire transcripts and repeated Not recorded lines.

Question-stage saves persist interview progress only. Explicit final text/status go through the native writer after confirmation; no automatic checklist/management-control changes, verification, prior-period conclusions, Review completion, Finding/Action creation/closure, or cross-criterion propagation. Preserve manual edits, cancellation, retry, concurrency, attribution, and history. New practices do not establish operation during an earlier Type 2 period.
